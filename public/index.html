About this time machine

Each day, 100 Years Ago Today subtracts exactly one century from the calendar and opens the newspapers printed on that date. The pages are real scans of the originals.

How to use it

  • Tap a page to open it. Pinch, double-tap or scroll to zoom, and drag to pan.
  • Turn on the magnifier, then press and hold on the page to read small print under a magnifying glass.
  • If no newspapers are in the archive for a day, the app tells you so.
  • Use the arrows or the date list to visit up to seven days either side of today's date, a century back.
  • Add the app to your home screen from your browser menu.

Where the pages come from

All pages come from Chronicling America, the Library of Congress collection of digitized historic American newspapers, built with the National Endowment for the Humanities through the National Digital Newspaper Program. Every page links back to its original record. Coverage varies by state and year, and many early papers were weeklies, so some days have few or no pages.

Historical newspapers carry the language and prejudices of their time, including advertisements connected to slavery. They are shown as printed.
